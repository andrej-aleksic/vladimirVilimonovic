VLADIMIR VILIMONOVIC

EXTREME ATHLETE & PHOTOGRAPHER

My name is Vladimir Vilimonovic. I am an athlete and adventure photographer with a passion for pushing boundaries and capturing the raw beauty of the outdoors. What began as a hoby on an amateur level has evolved into a serious pursuit of extreme sports and storytelling through photography. The search for adrenaline-fueled activities has become my everyday life. My work is focused on outdoor sports, photographing them, and showcasing the beauty that these sports reveal. The skills I have acquired allow me to access some hard-to-reach locations. Over the years, I have combined my passion for nature, travel, outdoor sports, and the art of photography, creating a unique approach that takes me to the heart of action and adrenaline. My specialties include paragliding expeditions, skiing, canyoning, running and major sporting events.

Feel free to contact me.
